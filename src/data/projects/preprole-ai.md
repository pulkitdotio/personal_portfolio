## Overview

PrepRole AI compares a candidate profile, resume, and job description to support personalized interview preparation. Google Gemini provides the AI analysis.

## Capabilities

- Candidate, resume, and job-description analysis with a role match score.
- Personalized technical questions and behavioral interview preparation.
- Skill-gap analysis and a personalized preparation plan.
- PDF resume upload and text extraction.
- AI-tailored ATS-friendly resume generation.
- Interview history and dashboard analytics.
- Registration, login, protected routes, session revocation, and account deletion.
- Structured validation of AI output before storing it.

## Supporting technologies

React 19, Vite, React Router, Axios, Sass, Lucide React, Lenis, Mongoose, JWT, bcryptjs, Zod, Multer, pdf-parse, Puppeteer, Helmet, and express-rate-limit.
